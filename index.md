I am a Finnish regional scientist with an academic background in the fields of human geography and sociology. I am
currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Karelian Institute](https://www.uef.fi/en/unit/karelian-institute) of the University of Eastern Finland,
contributing to various EU and national-level projects on regional and rural development. My recent work has
encompassed subjects such as sustainability transitions in rural areas (Horizon Europe RUSTIK), inter-regional relations
in Europe (ESPON IRiE) and stakeholder collaboration in regional-level place marketing (PhD research). Throughout my research career, I have developed extensive skills in coordinating and implementing EU, national, and local-level research projects. My methodological expertise covers carrying out qualitative case studies, in-depth interviews, and policy analysis.
