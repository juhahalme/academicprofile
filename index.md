I am a Finnish regional scientist with an academic background in human geography and sociology. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the [Karelian Institute](https://www.uef.fi/en/unit/karelian-institute) of the University of Eastern Finland, contributing to various EU and national-level projects. 

Throughout my research career, I have explored topics such as the sustainability transitions of rural areas, inter-regional relations in Europe and stakeholder collaboration in regional place branding projects.
