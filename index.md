I am a Finnish social scientist specialising in regional and rural development. I currently serve as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Karelian Institute](https://www.uef.fi/en/unit/karelian-institute), University of Eastern Finland, where I contribute to multiple EU and national projects. My present work is part of the Horizon Europe-funded project [RUSTIK](https://rustik-he.eu/), which examines rural sustainability transitions through integrated knowledge and policy improvement.
