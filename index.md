I am a Finnish social scientist currently working as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Karelian Institute](https://www.uef.fi/en/unit/karelian-institute), University of Eastern Finland. My research is part of the Horizon Europe project [RUSTIK](https://rustik-he.eu/) – Rural Sustainability Transitions through Integration of Knowledge for Improved Policy Processes. I earned my [doctoral degree](https://erepo.uef.fi/handle/123456789/23804) in 2020 with a dissertation focused on stakeholder collaboration in regional place marketing initiatives. My current academic interests lie in sustainability transitions, regional and rural policy, and inter-organizational collaboration—particularly within the framework of living labs. My work has been presented at numerous [international conferences]({% link presentations.md %}) and published in leading journals, including Environment and Planning C: Politics and Space, Qualitative Market Research, Place Branding and Public Diplomacy, and the Journal of Place Management and Development.
